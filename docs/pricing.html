Frequently Asked

Is the programme module included in every plan?

Yes. Every plan includes the programme module with Gantt, critical path, baselines, the programme assistant with voice input, import from MS Project, Asta or Primavera, and export for MS Project or Primavera. The plan sets how many programmes, baselines, imports and assistant requests you get: Free includes 1 programme of up to 150 tasks, Essential 10 programmes, and Professional and Scale unlimited programmes, each of up to 2,000 tasks.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan gives you 3 projects, 1 team member and the programme module with 1 programme of up to 150 tasks. Upgrade any time as your needs grow.

How does the free trial work?

Essential and Professional start with a 30-day free trial, and no credit card is needed. Scale starts through our sales team. If the trial ends without payment, your account falls back to the Free plan limits. Nothing is deleted.

Are there any per-user fees?

No. Each plan includes a set number of team members, and the Scale plan includes unlimited team members. No hidden per-seat charges.

How does the price compare with per-user programme licences?

Praevius charges one monthly price per company, not a licence for each user. Each plan includes a set number of team members: 1 on Free, 3 on Essential, 10 on Professional and unlimited on Scale. Adding a planner, site manager or QS to the team costs nothing extra until you reach the plan limit. Many scheduling tools are licensed per user, so compare the total cost for your whole team with the plan that fits.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. To change a paid plan, write to support@praevius.app. If you cancel, your plan stays active until the end of the billing period, and you can reactivate it before that date.

Is annual billing available?

Yes. Annual billing gives you 2 months free: Essential $990 per year, Professional $2,290 per year and Scale $4,490 per year, in USD.

What integrations are included?

Essential includes Google Drive Sync, SharePoint Document Sync and one accounting integration: Xero Invoicing, QuickBooks Online or Sage Intacct. Professional and Scale include all three accounting integrations. Scale adds Procore ↔︎ SharePoint Sync and Schedule of Values.

What is Agent Access?

Agent Access connects your own AI agent to the Praevius MCP server. Agent access is available over MCP with OAuth sign-in; read on every plan, write actions need Professional or Scale and your confirmation.

What’s included in support?

Paid plans include live support from our team by Microsoft Teams, phone or WhatsApp, including help with your first programme import. Every plan includes email support at support@praevius.app.

Larger Team? Talk to Us
