About Praevius: Programme Software from Construction Professionals

Talk to your programme. Speak or type your changes, and see the result on screen.

The Story

Praevius (from Latin “praevius”, meaning “going before”) started with a simple observation: on most projects, the programme is the document everyone depends on and the fewest people can update.

After two decades working as quantity surveyors across the UK, Australia and Poland, we saw the same pattern on project after project. Subcontractors and site teams were held back by:

  • Desktop planning licences priced per user, so only one or two people in the company could open or change the programme
  • Programmes arriving in different formats: MS Project from one main contractor, Asta Powerproject from the next, Primavera from a third
  • Slow weekly updates: progress and date changes collected on site, then keyed in later by the one person with the software

Praevius is our answer: construction programme software in the browser that you can talk to. Import the programme you already have, update it by voice or keyboard, check the critical path, and export it for MS Project or Primavera. One price for the company, with no per-user fees.

Import, Update by Voice, Check, Export

Import an MS Project, Asta Powerproject or Primavera file. It arrives as a new draft, with a report of what was carried and what was not.

Update by voice or keyboard. Click the mic, say “push frame out a week”, check the live transcript, and the change appears in the Gantt chart. Replies are on screen, and typing always works.

Check the critical path, float and baseline comparison, and run Validate for open ends and negative float.

Export for MS Project (XML) or Primavera (XER), or to PDF and Excel, with a report of anything that could not be carried.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who built Praevius?

Praevius was built by quantity surveyors with 20 years in construction across the UK, Australia and Poland. The software comes from people who have worked with construction programmes, cost reports and progress claims on real projects.

What is Praevius used for?

Praevius is construction programme software you can talk to. You import your MS Project, Asta Powerproject or Primavera programme, update it by voice or keyboard in the browser, check the critical path and float, and export it for MS Project or Primavera. Cost control is a separate module on the same platform, on the Essential plan and above.

Who is Praevius for?

Subcontractors, package planners, site managers, small main contractors and QS practices. Praevius suits subcontractor, package and mid-size main-contractor programmes of up to 2,000 tasks. It does not do resource or cost loading.

Does the AI change my programme without asking?

Ordinary edits apply at once and you can undo them. Larger or destructive changes, such as deleting more than 10 tasks, imports, restores or deleting a programme, are proposed first and run only when you confirm.

Is Praevius related to BIM Takeoff?

Yes. Praevius is the programme and cost platform of the BIM Takeoff ecosystem. BIM Takeoff (bimtakeoff.com) provides quantity takeoff and estimating services.
